How AI Matching Works

A step-by-step technical breakdown of our NLP TF-IDF, Cosine Similarity, and Computer Vision Feature Correlation Pipeline.

1
Item Reporting

User submits lost or found item details, including category, description, color, brand, location, date, and photo.

2
Text Preprocessing

Cleans text by stripping punctuation, lowercasing, and filtering English stop-words to standardize the corpus.

3
TF-IDF Vectorization

Converts unstructured description text into high-dimensional numerical TF-IDF feature vectors using unigrams and bigrams.

4
Cosine Similarity

Computes the dot product of normalized TF-IDF vector angles: sim(A,B) = (A·B) / (||A||*||B||).

Configurable Attribute Weights

The overall match confidence score is computed as a weighted linear combination of six individual attribute similarity metrics defined in config.py:

Matching Dimension Algorithm / Technique Weight
Text & Description TF-IDF + Cosine Similarity 35%
Item Category Exact & Taxonomy Hierarchy 20%
Color & Brand Token Intersection & Jaccard 15%
Location Proximity SequenceMatcher Fuzzy Ratio 15%
Date & Time Exponential Day Decay Function 10%
Image Features OpenCV HSV Color Histogram Correlation 5%

Dynamic Match Formula

Total Score = (35% × NLP_Text_Sim)
             + (20% × Category_Sim)
             + (15% × Color_Brand_Sim)
             + (15% × Location_Sim)
             + (10% × Date_Decay_Sim)
             + (5% × OpenCV_Vision_Sim)
Score Classification Tiers
90% – 100%: Very High Match Green Badge
75% – 89%: High Match Teal Badge
50% – 74%: Possible Match Yellow Badge
Below 50%: Low Match Secondary Badge
